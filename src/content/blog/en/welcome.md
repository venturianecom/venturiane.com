Venturian Ecom deliberately starts small. No grand claims or complicated
layers, just a place for practical work around technology and e-commerce.

## What this place is for

Short notes about building, improving and experimenting will appear here. About
technical decisions, e-commerce and the lessons learned along the way.

## Built to grow

The website is simple, but not temporary. New articles are written in Markdown
and automatically turned into fast static pages. This keeps the content easy to
maintain and pleasant to read.
